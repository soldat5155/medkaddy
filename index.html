MedKaddy

A simple medication tracker with large buttons and clear text, made for older adults and the family members who help them.

  • Log each dose with one tap, and see at a glance what's been taken today.
  • Reminders, refill warnings, and a dose history to share with your doctor.
  • Family sharing: a caregiver can log doses and get an alert if one is missed.

Made for Kady, and for everyone who's ever wondered "did I already take that?"

MedKaddy is a record-keeping tool, not medical advice. Always follow your doctor, your pharmacist, and your medication label. In an emergency, call 911.
