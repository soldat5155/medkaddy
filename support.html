Support

Need help with MedKaddy? Email medkaddy.support@gmail.com and describe what happened. For iCloud or sharing problems, it helps to include the details from My Medications → Backup and iCloud → Sync details (tap Copy details).

Common questions

How do I undo a dose I logged by mistake?

Tap Undo right after logging, or tap the medication again and choose "I didn't take it. Remove it." You can also remove doses in History.

How do I share with a family member?

Go to My Medications → Caregivers and send an invitation. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud. Caregiver sharing is part of Premium; only the person sharing needs Premium.

How do I restore my medications on a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account and keep "Keep a copy in my iCloud" on. Your medications come back automatically. You can also restore from a backup file under Backup and iCloud.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose MedKaddy. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

MedKaddy can't give medical advice. For questions about your medications, ask your doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency, call 911.
